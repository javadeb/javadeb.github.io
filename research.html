Research

PhD Researcher at KU Leuven working on electromagnetic systems, wireless communications, machine learning for RF systems, biomedical microwave sensing, and next-generation channel modeling.

Research Areas

  • Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence
  • Electromagnetic Wave Propagation
  • Neural Networks for CSI Feedback and Channel Extrapolation
  • Inverse Electromagnetic Problems
  • Microwave Sensors and Biomedical Applications
  • Ray Tracing and RCS-Based Propagation Modeling
