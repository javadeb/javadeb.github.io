Research

PhD researcher at KU Leuven working on applied electromagnetics, microwave imaging, wireless propagation, RF sensing, machine learning for communication systems, and inverse electromagnetic problems.

Research Interests

  • Microwave Imaging
  • Electromagnetic Modeling
  • Machine Learning for RF Systems
  • Wireless Channel Modeling
  • Inverse Problems and Signal Processing
  • Microwave Heating and Dielectric Material Characterization
  • Neural Networks for Channel Extrapolation
  • Millimeter-Wave Propagation and Ray Tracing
  • Biomedical Microwave Sensing
