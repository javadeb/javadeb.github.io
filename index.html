About Me

With a comprehensive educational journey culminating in a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from KU Leuven, where I also served as a Research Assistant, I bring extensive knowledge and experience in Wireless Communications, channel modeling, neural networks, RF systems, electromagnetics (EM), microwave technologies, and antenna design. Throughout my academic and professional experiences, I have honed my skills in designing and simulating millimeter-wave (MmWave) wireless channels using ray tracing techniques and developing channel state information feedback systems powered by neural networks. My expertise spans various RF components, from antennas to front-end modules, utilizing EM solvers like HFSS and CST, along with circuit simulators such as ADS. My research has focused on wireless propagation, neural networks in RF and microwave applications, and phased array antennas. This diverse background enables me to model various RF architectures in MATLAB Simulink or ADS/Systemview, optimizing the performance, reliability, and efficiency of RF products. In my current role as an RF Designer at MEAM, I specialize in designing high-efficiency microwave sources using klystron and magnetron lamps. By leveraging tools like COMSOL and HFSS, I design and simulate high-power EM sources at L-band and C-band frequencies. My collaborative approach with engineers and researchers has significantly contributed to enhancing the performance, reliability, and efficiency of high-power EM sources. Driven by a passion for advancing RF and microwave technologies, I am excited about the prospect of applying my diverse skills in a dynamic environment. I value diversity, innovation, and collaboration, and I am confident that my unique perspective and background would make a valuable contribution to the team
