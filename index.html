About Me

Hi, I’m Javad Ebrahimizadeh, an RF and Microwave Engineer at MEAM in Belgium, with a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from KU Leuven. My research and engineering activities focus on electromagnetics, wireless communication systems, millimeter-wave propagation, and machine learning for RF systems.

During my PhD at KU Leuven, I worked extensively on wireless propagation modeling, ray tracing, massive MIMO systems, and active phased-array antennas. My work combines physics-based electromagnetic modeling with data-driven machine learning approaches for next-generation wireless systems.

I have experience in millimeter-wave channel characterization, RCS-based propagation modeling, antenna design, microwave sensing, and biomedical RF applications. My research also includes microwave imaging systems, inverse electromagnetic problems, and non-invasive sensing technologies.

I actively work with advanced electromagnetic and RF simulation tools including HFSS, CST Studio Suite, Altair FEKO, ADS, COMSOL Multiphysics, and MATLAB/Python. These tools allow me to work from antenna and RF front-end design to system-level analysis and optimization.

Currently, I work on high-efficiency microwave sources and high-power electromagnetic systems operating in L-band and C-band frequencies. My current engineering activities involve microwave heating systems, magnetron-based RF systems, and industrial microwave applications.

I am passionate about advancing RF and microwave technologies, particularly in areas where electromagnetics, machine learning, and wireless sensing intersect. I strongly value innovation, collaboration, and continuous learning.
