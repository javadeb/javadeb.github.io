Javad Ebrahimizadeh - RF and Microwave Engineer

Hi, I’m Javad Ebrahimizadeh, an RF and Microwave Engineer at MEAM in Belgium, with a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from KU Leuven. My work focuses on electromagnetics, wireless communication systems, millimeter-wave propagation, RF engineering, and machine learning for RF systems.

During my PhD at KU Leuven, I worked extensively on wireless propagation modeling, ray tracing, massive MIMO systems, active phased-array antennas, and electromagnetic simulation techniques. My research combines physics-based electromagnetic modeling with data-driven machine learning approaches for next-generation wireless systems.

I also have experience in microwave sensing, biomedical RF applications, microwave imaging systems, inverse electromagnetic problems, antenna design, and non-invasive sensing technologies. My work includes developing microwave systems for biomedical imaging, sensing, and industrial RF applications.

Currently, I work on high-efficiency microwave sources, high-power electromagnetic systems, microwave heating technologies, and industrial microwave applications operating mainly in L-band and C-band frequencies.

Background

My academic and engineering background is centered around RF, microwave engineering, electromagnetics, wireless communications, and applied machine learning. Over the years, I have worked on both theoretical and practical aspects of electromagnetic systems, ranging from antenna and RF front-end design to system-level wireless propagation analysis and optimization.

I have experience working in research-oriented environments as well as industrial engineering projects involving RF hardware, microwave systems, electromagnetic simulations, and data-driven modeling approaches.
