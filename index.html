About Javad Ebrahimizadeh

Hi, I'm Javad Ebrahimizadeh, an RF and Microwave Engineer at MEAM in Belgium, holding a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from KU Leuven. My work spans electromagnetics, wireless communication systems, millimeter-wave propagation, and machine learning for RF systems.

During my PhD at KU Leuven I worked extensively on wireless propagation modeling, ray tracing, massive MIMO, active phased-array antennas, and electromagnetic simulation. My research merges physics-based EM modeling with data-driven machine learning approaches for next-generation wireless networks.

I also have hands-on experience in microwave sensing, biomedical RF applications, microwave imaging, inverse electromagnetic problems, and non-invasive sensing. Currently I work on high-efficiency microwave sources, high-power EM systems, microwave heating, and industrial RF applications in L-band and C-band.
