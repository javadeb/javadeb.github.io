About Me

Hi, I’m Javad Ebrahimizadeh, an RF and microwave engineer at MEAM company in Belgium, with a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from KU Leuven, where I also worked as a Research Assistant. My work lies at the intersection of electromagnetics, wireless communications, and machine learning, with a focus on understanding and improving how signals behave in complex environments. Over the years, I’ve developed a strong background in wireless propagation, channel modeling, and RF system design. I’ve worked extensively on millimeter-wave channel simulations using ray tracing techniques, as well as on neural network–based methods for channel state information and system optimization. I particularly enjoy combining physics-based models with data-driven approaches to design smarter and more efficient RF systems. My experience spans a wide range of tools and technologies, including electromagnetic solvers such as HFSS and CST, as well as circuit and system-level platforms like ADS and MATLAB. This allows me to move seamlessly from antenna and front-end design to full system modeling and performance evaluation. Currently, I work as an RF Designer, focusing on high-efficiency microwave sources based on magnetron and klystron technologies. Using tools like COMSOL and HFSS, I design and simulate high-power electromagnetic systems operating in L-band and C-band frequencies. I’m passionate about advancing RF and microwave technologies, especially where they intersect with machine learning and sensing. I value curiosity, collaboration, and continuous learning—and I’m always excited to explore new ideas and challenges.
